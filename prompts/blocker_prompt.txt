For each blocker, extract:
1. **ID**: Generate a unique identifier (e.g., "BLK-001", "BLK-002")
2. **Description**: Clear description of the blocker or risk
3. **Type**: Category of blocker (technical/resource/dependency/external/approval)

   - Dependency: Waiting on other teams, systems, or deliverables
   - External: Third-party vendors, clients, external factors
   - Approval: Waiting for decisions, sign-offs, permissions
4. **Severity**: Impact level (critical/high/medium/low)
   - Critical: Completely blocks progress, immediate action required
   - High: Significantly impacts timeline or quality
   - Medium: Causes delays but workarounds possible
   - Low: Minor inconvenience, minimal impact
5. **Blocking**: What or who is causing the block
6. **Owner**: Person responsible for resolving the blocker
7. **Impact**: Description of how this affects the project/timeline
8. **Mentioned At**: Timestamp when the blocker was mentioned (if available)
